Warnings a district can actually use

Flood and landslide early warning for the districts that flood first, presented as one dashboard a district administration can act on.

TL;DR: Flood warnings exist as raw data and PDFs. District teams need one screen: where, how bad, what to do next. AapdaSetu is that screen, and it has already been proposed to a district administration.

Context

Chhattisgarh and Bihar flood every year, and the warning chain breaks at the last mile: data exists, but it does not arrive as a decision. Swagat leads the Chhattisgarh chapter of NYCC India, so this problem is local, not abstract.

What was built

DisasterAI predicts flood and landslide risk; AapdaSetu presents it as a district-ready dashboard. A full package went to the Khagaria District Administration on 14 November 2026: a 9-page proposal, a tailored resume and a cover email. An interactive demo, built directly from the proposal with all data labelled simulated, followed on 21 November 2026.

Status

The package and demo are with the district. The next step is the district's demo invitation, and the walkthrough is ready to present.
