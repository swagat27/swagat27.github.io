AapdaSetu

disaster early warning project

AapdaSetu is the product form of DisasterAI: flood and landslide early warning for districts that flood first, presented as a dashboard a district administration can actually use. It began at Samsung Solve for Tomorrow and became a full proposal for the Khagaria District Administration in Bihar.

10 minguided demo walkthrough
1district proposal submitted
2hazards: flood and landslide

The problem

Flood warnings exist as raw data and PDFs. District teams need one screen: where, how bad, what to do next, in the local context.

What was built

An early warning concept with risk prediction for Chhattisgarh's flood-prone districts, a district-ready dashboard, and an interactive demo built directly from the written proposal, with all data clearly labelled simulated.

Status

The full package (9-page proposal, tailored resume, cover email) was sent to the Khagaria District Administration on 14 November 2026, and the interactive demo followed on 21 November 2026. Awaiting the district's demo invitation.
