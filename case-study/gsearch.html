A CAD aggregator for mechanical components: one search across the scattered vendor libraries an engineer otherwise opens one by one.

Context

As Vice-Captain of Team Agnishul, Swagat hunted components under deadline. The parts were never the problem; the dozen differently-shaped search boxes were.

What was built

A single aggregator interface over mechanical component libraries, designed by its own heaviest user, then rebuilt properly in Next.js 15 and published live on GitHub Pages.

What it shows

A tool born from a real workflow, designed from the user's seat, and rebuilt to a modern stack instead of being abandoned.
