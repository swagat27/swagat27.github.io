About Swagat Baghel

Swagat Baghel is a final-year B.Tech Mechanical Engineering student at IIT BHILAI (graduating May 2027), based in Bengaluru, Karnataka, India. He works as a Business Functions Intern at Myntra and builds AI products including BoxPilot, GSearch and ToneShift. He was a Research Fellow at IIT Bombay and is Vice-Captain of Team Agnishul, the SAE M-Baja team at IIT BHILAI.
