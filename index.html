Swagat Baghel

a Business Functions Intern at Myntra with a background in mechanical engineering.

— who's curious about how things actually work, and how they could work better.

A final-year mechanical engineering student (IIT BHILAI '27) working on category analytics for Men's Apparel: assortment gaps, pricing, search tooling like SearchIQ, and account analytics for a portfolio of fashion brands. Before this, a Research Fellow at IIT Bombay, where my topology optimization work made 3D printed concrete structures 30% lighter. On my own time, I build AI products.

I grew up in Odisha, in a small town most people have not heard of, and took the long way here: core engineering, a race car team, a research lab, and now a fashion company in Bengaluru. Since I was a kid, I have always been intrigued by one question: 'How does this actually work, and who decides?' That question stayed with me through an engineering degree, a research fellowship, and now, as I work in business and build products. I am still learning how to answer it. I am Swagat, an engineer learning business by doing it.
