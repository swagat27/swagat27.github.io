About

I am Swagat Baghel, a final-year B.Tech Mechanical Engineering student at IIT BHILAI (graduating May 2027), currently a Business Functions Intern at Myntra in Bengaluru. My permanent home is Nuapada, Odisha. My days sit in category management for Men's Apparel: assortment, pricing, search analytics and brand accounts.

The longer version: I started in core engineering, fabrication and motorsport, as Vice-Captain of Team Agnishul, our SAE M-Baja team of 50+ members. A research fellowship at IIT Bombay followed, on BESO topology optimization for 3D concrete printing, which cut structural mass by 30%. Then a Siemens virtual internship in systems simulation, a UI/UX internship at Noqs Digital where I was named Star Intern, and the Myntra internship, which moved me to Bengaluru and into business.

In parallel, I build AI products alone: BoxPilot, ToneShift, GSearch, CallToDeck, AutoForm and DisasterAI. I lead the Chhattisgarh chapters of NYCC India and YCIC (YuWaah-UNICEF), represent my department on the Student Placement Team, mentor 8 juniors, host the Success Speaks podcast, and film it all on YouTube as @Swagat-IITBH.
